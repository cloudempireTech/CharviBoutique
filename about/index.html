Charvi Boutique

Rooted in Kolkata.
Drawn to detail.

Charvi Boutique has offered ethnic wear in Kolkata since 2009. Its collection includes sarees, salwars, kurtis and bed covers, with a particular focus on handloom materials.

In the boutique, fabric takes centre stage: the lightness of mulmul, the familiarity of tant, the texture of tussar and the soft sheen of Chanderi or Dupion silk. Visit to see the current selection in person.
