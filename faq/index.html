Where is Charvi Boutique?

The boutique is listed at 4B Baman Para Lane, near Ballygunge Phari, Ballygunge, Kolkata, West Bengal 700019.

What does Charvi Boutique sell?

The known range includes sarees, salwars, kurtis and bed covers. Its saree selection includes handloom materials such as cotton, tussar, Chanderi and Dupion silk.

Can I see current pieces online?

The gallery shows past displays. For current pieces, colours or availability, please call or message the boutique directly.

How can I contact the boutique?

Call +91 98311 32890 or +91 94772 25097. You can also send a WhatsApp message to the primary number.

Do you list prices or online checkout?

Prices and online checkout are not provided here. Please contact the boutique for details on a piece you are interested in.

Can I visit in person?

Yes. Use the address and directions on our contact page. Calling ahead is a good way to confirm the latest opening times and availability.
